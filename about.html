About Happy Tails Adoption

At Happy Tails Adoption, our mission is simple yet powerful: to rescue, nurture, and find loving forever homes for abandoned and homeless dogs across Nepal.

We collaborate closely with local shelters, volunteers, and communities to provide care, medical attention, and socialization for dogs in need. Each dog we save gets a second chance at happiness and companionship.

Whether you're looking to adopt, foster, or support our mission through volunteering or donations, your involvement helps transform lives. Together, we’re building a kinder, safer world for our furry friends.

Join Happy Tails Adoption and become part of a compassionate community dedicated to making tails wag and hearts full.
