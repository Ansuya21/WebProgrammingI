About Us

At Happy Tails Adoption, we believe every dog deserves a chance at love and happiness. Our mission is to rescue, care for, and find forever homes for abandoned and homeless dogs across Nepal. We work with dedicated volunteers and families to create happy endings, one tail at a time. 🐾

Whether you’re looking to adopt, foster, or support our cause, your contribution makes a big difference. Join us in giving hope and love to these wonderful companions. ❤️
