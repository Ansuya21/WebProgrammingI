Welcome to Presidential Graduate School

Presidential Graduate School is one of Nepal's premier institutions for higher education in IT and business. With a commitment to academic excellence and modern learning environments, we aim to empower students with the skills needed to thrive in a rapidly evolving digital world. Our programs are developed in collaboration with industry experts to ensure that students graduate with both theoretical knowledge and hands-on experience. 

Programs Offered

BSc.IT (Bachelor of Science in Information Technology) – A comprehensive 4-year program focusing on programming, databases, networking, and IoT.
MBA (Master of Business Administration) – A two-year business leadership program integrating theory and practice.
We also offer certificate programs, internships, and career guidance services.
